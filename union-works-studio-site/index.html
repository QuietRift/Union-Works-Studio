03 / Process

Built like a system, not a template.

Every project starts with the business problem. The visual design comes after the structure is right.

01Audit

Business, customers, current site, proof, competitors, technical constraints, and where the presentation is breaking down.

02Direction

Positioning, information architecture, content priorities, and the visual system that best fits the business.

03Design

High-fidelity responsive interface work with real content and deliberate interaction decisions.

04Build

Production implementation, responsive behavior, accessibility, integrations, content, and technical QA.

05Ship

Launch, SEO fundamentals, analytics readiness, handoff, and an optional support plan if the project needs one.

04 / Studio principles
01
No fake proof.

No invented client counts, fake testimonials, fabricated analytics, or vanity metrics. The work carries the credibility.

02
Templates are raw material.

A good foundation can save time. It should never be obvious what template the final business started from.

03
Build for the next click.

Navigation, hierarchy, content, and interaction should make the customer’s next decision easier—not merely make the page prettier.

04
Ship something maintainable.

The finished site should be understandable, editable, responsive, and clean enough to keep improving after launch.

Union Works / Field note 001

Your business can look better online without pretending to be bigger than it is.
